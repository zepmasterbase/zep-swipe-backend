Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What is Zep Swipe?

Zep Swipe is a platform focused on the student economy, helping people explore marketplace services, deals and mobility enquiries.

Can I explore before creating an account?

You can visit the marketplace and mobility pages first. Some actions and account features may require registration or sign-in.

How do I offer my services?

Open the sign-up page, select the Student Provider account type if available, and follow the registration steps.

How do I join as a business?

Create an account and select the Business account type if available in the registration page.

How do I submit a mobility enquiry?

Open Mobility, complete the enquiry form and review the consent notice before submitting your details.

Where can I manage my account?

Use the My Account link to open the account page. If you are not signed in, follow the sign-in instructions shown there.
